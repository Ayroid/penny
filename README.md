# Penny

Track today. Build tomorrow.

Penny is a personal money tracker for Android. It records what comes in, what
goes out, and what moves between your accounts, then shows where the money
went. Everything stays on your phone.

- **Add in seconds.** The amount field opens with the number pad already up,
  and everything else has a sensible default.
- **Save what you repeat.** Mark a transaction for quick add and it becomes a
  chip behind the add button, ready for next time.
- **Accounts and transfers.** Moving cash out of the bank isn't spending, and
  the month's totals know that.
- **Categories** you can rename, recolour and archive, each with its own icon.
- **Recurring entries** for rent, salary and subscriptions. They catch up the
  next time you open the app.
- **Insights.** Credit against debit over time, what changed since last time,
  and a breakdown by category as a list or a pie chart. Look at a day, a week,
  a month, or any range you pick.
- **Goals** you save towards, with progress you can see.
- **Hide the numbers.** One tap masks every amount until you tap it again.
- **Local and quiet.** No sign-in, no sync, no analytics. Your backup is a file
  you export and keep.

## Install

Download the latest APK from
[Releases](https://github.com/Ayroid/penny/releases/latest). After that, use
Settings → Check for updates to get new versions.
